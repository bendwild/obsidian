I realize now
I never understood love

I never fully understood how the union between a man and a woman manifests
how the male and female psyche interlock
I never fully believed in gender differences
is it not just how we were socialized?
an incomplete truth

the boomer statements
how the man is the provider
and needs to be the peace and stability of the family
finally make sense

Not a provider in a purely material way
as a big part of our dating culture seems to believe

but you provide your ear
listen intently
as nothing makes her happier 
than yapping about the lows and highs of life

you provide your heart
unwavering love
no matter what happens
this is your commitment

you provide your labor
make sure to plan dates
clean the dishes, fix up the house
so her mind can be in peace

you provide safety
physically, emotionally, financially
a place for her to thrive
and fall back when she falls

this is your role
take it seriously
and you will be reap the rewards a hundredfold
not because love is transactional
but because love alone is not enough

to love
is to feel heard, loved, taken care of and safe
and so much more
