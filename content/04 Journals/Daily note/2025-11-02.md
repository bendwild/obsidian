i want to buy you flowers
dark red tulips cuz they remind me of your lips
pastel roses as soft as your skin
violet hyacinths, purple as your space in my heart
and maybe as a reminder of your smile, the biggest sunflower i can find

i want to surprise you with my presence
a gentle massage after a long day
homemade cooking on a saturday night
surprise dates in the parisian sunset
and maybe a kiss on the cheek, as you sleep the night away

i want to hold you
your soft hands as we stroll through a botanic garden
your glistening hair as it falls in front of your eyes
your waist as i playfully dance with you in our kitchen
and maybe the keys to your heart, so i don’t have to knock

i’ve waited long
and will have to wait even longer
but oh how lucky i am to have someone to turn my patience to

soon my love
we’ll be together
more than we’ve ever been
