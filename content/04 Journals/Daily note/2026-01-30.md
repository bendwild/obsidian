love is a funny thing
some say it can move mountains
i would argue
*it makes distance blossom*

the little celebrations
morning affirmations
and spontaneous flirtations
*it makes distance blossom*

cuteness
whether it be you
kittens, dogs…
or even a phallic plushie
*it makes distance blossom*

as i write this
i find myself inspired
by the person who has captured my heart
mercilessly, and with military precision
her loving nature and bottomless vigour
*it makes distance blossom*

i apologize for copying your line
but the simplicity and beauty of it
is something i could only dream of writing
and my spirit knows
the beauty of the line is temporary
since when the blossom bears it fruit
distance will be but recollections
fertilizer for our next bountiful harvest
