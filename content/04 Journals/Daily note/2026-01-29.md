My muscles tense
fingers hovering over the keys
with palpable anticipation
the perfect storm withers
as the blue light illuminates my struggle

i remind myself, the image
of the knight who mounts his steed
picks up his lance
and with little regard for life or liberty
throws himself at the adversary

such am i
in almost no sense
no steed, no lance
i like to live
with vague, theoretical adversaries

how i envy the knight
despite the festering violence
the simplicity of the fight
allures me
