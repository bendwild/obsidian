October 31st

I am walking home in the soft glow of sunset. The straps of my grocery bags holding my precious dinner ingredients cut into my hand without remorse. Pink pantheress whispers sweet words in my ears as I cross the street. A young couple passes me. One dressed as a zombie, the other one seems to be a scientist or doctor judging from the lab coat. I can only assume how proud they are of this brainy practical joke. 

As I continue my journey home, a variety of creatures pass me. A witch, a goblin, something resembling a ghoul… The more time passes, the more realistic the outfits become. I have never been a real Halloween person, but the effort that went into these outfits did not fail to impress me. I think I even saw someone phase through a building dressed in a loosely fitting white sheet, still have not figured out how they got that illusion to work. 

I arrive home. My hands are begging me for a break. I reach for the button of the elevator but bony fingers beat me to it. I look back and see a tall dark figure reach over me. “Oh, the grim reaper!”, I exclaim. I haven’t seen that today! He stares at me blankly. “So you out to get me today?”, I joke. No reaction… 

While the 10th floor has an amazing view, it does make for awkward elevator rides that seem to last for ages. As I count down the seconds refreshing my Instagram to seem busy, the figure stands tall with a serene but unnerving atmosphere. The sound of the elevator releases me. As I’m walking out I hear a voice behind me mumble “November 7th”. Before I could turn around, the doors that were holding me hostage just a few seconds ago closed on me, as overly involved bouncers in a nightclub. 

Weirdo, i whispered under my breath, assuring myself it was just an average interaction with someone who has definitely smoked something. 

Somehow, I don’t look forward to next week.
