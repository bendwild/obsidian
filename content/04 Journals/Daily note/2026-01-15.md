My dearest,

As thou once proclaimed
*“i mean, i like dick, but i also like poetry”*
A phrase so subtle and profound
it would be wholly inappropriate
not to reciprocate

Allow me, love

I was not prepared for the profound intensity of love
Fully satisfied with my own felicity, you arrived
For since meeting you in the picturesque valleys of Austria
you have become the measure by which my days are judged
the comfort to which I look forward, the amusement I recall with unguarded pleasure,  
days without your presence linger with a restlessness
that only calms in the warmth of your voice

Pray forgive my faults
as my devotion proceeds with the recklessness of youth
My strongest conviction found in a shared future
for it now contains you,
a striped feline creature 
and sweet recollections i am unwilling to relinquish

Sometimes my thoughts betray me
with a violence that I can barely restrain
the sight of your wicked smile
unable to hide its mischievous intent
as our bodies move in unison
and my hands pull you closer
a tremble runs down our spines
entirely of my imagination
until distance has been abandoned

If love may be reasoned into existence,
then I find no argument against our affection
earnest and enduring
As I would be solemnly speaking nothing but the truth
that every small particular part of you
your kindness, your wit, the energy with which you occupy a room
has laid claim upon my heart
now home to the celestial creature
I hope one day to call my wife

With utmost sincerity, 
Yours.
