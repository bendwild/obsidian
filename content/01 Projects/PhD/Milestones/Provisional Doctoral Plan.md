> [!info] Instructions
> - The project must be written clearly and it must be understandable by scientists working outside the specific research field covered by the project
> - You can use a **maximum of 5 pages**  (use **Font size 12pt** with at least **single line spacing**)
> - You can use 1 or 2 figures or diagrams 
> - You can write the plan in Dutch or English.

  
**_Outline of the provisional doctoral plan_**

- **Title page** (= page 1)
    - **Title** (you can still modify the title of your thesis in KU Loket before you get imprimatur)
    - **Name of applicant**
    - Name, affiliation and e-mail addresses of **supervisor** **and** **co-supervisor(s)** 
    - **Location** where the research will be done (address for correspondence)
    - **Available grant support (FWO, IWT, ...) with dates**
    - **Number of Ethical Committee (if applicable)**
    - **Animal experiments? Yes/No**
	    - NO
    - [**Thematic programme**](https://gbiomed.kuleuven.be/english/phd/thematic-programmes)
	    - Generic trach (part-time researcher)
    - **Starting date ( = date of registration for the doctoral training, not predoc!)**
	    - 1/10
    - **Full-time or part-time PhD project**
	    - Part-time PhD project
    - **Joint PhD?  Home and Host Institution?**
    - **Expected end date**

- **Introduction and background** (= page 2)  
    Give a **brief** introduction with an overview of the general and specific background of the project, refer to recent and relevant review articles and identify gaps in the current knowledge.  
    **Always** indicate whether this is the continuation of an existing research line within the research unit or whether this is totally new research.  
    **In case of a joint PhD**, clearly specify the distribution of the research activities between the home and host institutions.

###### Please use pages 3 and 4 for the following sections (do not exceed 2 pages!)

- **General hypothesis and specific aims of the project**

- **Methodology**  
    Mention the techniques you will use. Clearly indicate:  
    **(a)** whether they are existing techniques or have to be developed as part of the PhD project;  
    **(b)** whether these techniques involve collaborations with third party labs;  
    **(c)** status of request sent to the ethical committee (if relevant);  
    **(d)** clearly indicate what you will be doing yourself, and what will be done for you.  
    **(e)** **clearly indicate numbers** (X patients, participants, animals, ...) in the experimental design.

- **Milestones and timing of PhD project**

Provide a concise description of the different phases of your PhD project, including a timeline with estimated completion dates. For part-time candidates: clarify any deviations from the standard schedule. In addition, outline the feasibility of the proposed project.

- **Conclusion**: e.g. the estimated end points/deliverables of the PhD projects.
- **If other PhDs are being performed around similar topics, please mention the titles and the name(s) of the PhD researcher(s) involved and the differences with your project.**
- **If data have been collected before the start of your PhD, please mention this in your provisional doctoral plan as the committee has to approve that these data will be used for your project.**

- **References** (= page 5)
