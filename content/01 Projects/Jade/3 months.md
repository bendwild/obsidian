can i tell you something
a story about a certain someone
who leaves me in awe
speechless

i think i might have found some words
it only took me 3 months…

it’s a funny story actually
a story about happy coincidences
tears of joy
and phallic humor

i hope i can make you laugh forever
it’s the kind of music i put on repeat

it’s a beautiful story
the kind that lingers
in the soft silence between pages
in the playful words we whisper

the kind so extra, as if to state
there are too many mediocre things in life

it’s our story
you, and me 
in every season
a cycle that will never grow old

we must have met in an other lifetime
3 months of loving came way too easily
