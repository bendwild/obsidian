Short and concise summary for true beginners. Was not advanced enough for me and remained very theoretical. 

# Highlights

One classic model is the 60/40 portfolio, which allocates 60% of your investments to stocks and 40% to bonds. This strategy aims to balance the growth potential of stocks with the stability of bonds, providing a moderate level of risk and return.
