Organized abandonment by capital and the state refers to the loss of protection from vulnerability that communities experience when safe housing, clean water, reliable jobs, healthy food, and social service provisions gradually disappear from towns and increased police presence and criminalization fill the cracks of a compromised social infrastructure.
