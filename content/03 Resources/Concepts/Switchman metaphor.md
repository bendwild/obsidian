[[@Max Weber]]

The "switchman" metaphor posits that while material and ideal interests directly govern human conduct, ideas—or "world images"—act like railway switchmen. They determine the tracks along which action is pushed by the dynamics of interest. Ideas shape the direction of historical change, rather than merely reflecting economic conditions.
