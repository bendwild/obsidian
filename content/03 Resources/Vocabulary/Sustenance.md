“The word ‘sustainability’ has its origins in ‘sustenance’, which in its etymology connects with the Latin word sustinere, which in turn means to furnish with support for survival.

The noun '**sustenance**' has its etymological origins in Latin and Old French. It is derived from the Latin word 'sustinere,' which is a combination of 'sub' (under) and 'tenere' (to hold). This Latin term conveys the idea of holding up or supporting from beneath. In Old French, it became '**sustenance**,' which referred to the act of sustaining or supporting. Over time, '**sustenance**' came to specifically denote food and drink that is necessary for maintaining life and health. It embodies the concept that these essentials support and sustain the well-being and vitality of an individual. The etymology of '**sustenance**' emphasizes its historical connection to the fundamental elements required to nourish and sustain life, underscoring the critical importance of food and drink in human existence.
