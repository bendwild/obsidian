With the 2026 Lancet Countdown Report on Health and Climate Change launched on October 28, 2026, this special debrief for health professionals provided an opportunity to hear directly about the report’s latest findings and consider what they mean for health professionals, health systems, and climate action at national and global levels.  
  
Health system resilience and the active engagement of health professionals are central to effective climate action. The health workforce has a unique role in advancing climate mitigation and adaptation—by advocating for evidence-based policies, leading sustainable healthcare practices, and protecting vulnerable populations from climate-related health threats. Building on the findings of the Lancet Countdown, this special debrief session will provide an in-depth look at the latest data and trends, explore strategies to strengthen health system resilience, and discuss the pivotal role of health professionals in driving climate and health action. Participants will engage in dialogue on country experiences, good practices, and lessons learned in integrating health into climate strategies and fostering a sustainable and healthy future for all.

● Dr Yasna Palmeiro-Silva, Lancet Countdown on Health and Climate Change
