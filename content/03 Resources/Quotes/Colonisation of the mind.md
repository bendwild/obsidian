A process where the coloniser imposes not just practical systems of control but also, through language, imagery, and branding, manufactures a worldview that reshapes how the colonised person perceives themselves. It is a rebranding of a population’s sense of self and identity. And when that manufactured worldview is internalised, it becomes a kind of psychological captivity.

[[@Frantz Fanon]]
