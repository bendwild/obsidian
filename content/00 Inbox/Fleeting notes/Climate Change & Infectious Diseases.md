# Overall impact
## Increased incidence, morbidity & mortality

> Climate change has increased the risk of over half of all known infectious diseases

The ability of pathogens to survive, reproduce, and spread is shaped by: 
- environmental conditions
- population dynamics
- opportunities that facilitate transmission
WHICH ARE ALL AFFECTED BY CLIMATE CHANGE

## Increased exposure and spread

> Climate change is increasing the risk of pathogen exposures and facilitating pathogen spread

Climate change induces: 
- the creation of favorable environments for pathogen success
- shifting geographic range of pathogens to reach more of new populations
- mobilizing pathogens to spread quickly and efficiently
- creating secondary effects that increase exposure opportunities

# Water & Health

|               | IMPACT                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Flooding**      | - increased opportunistic infections from cold, wet living conditions<br>- more diarrhea outbreaks from contaminated flood waters<br>- increased incidence of [[malaria]] from standing flood waters<br>- all of these contribute to worsen [[HIV]] health                       |
| **Precipitation** | Half of the world population living in water-stressed areas, impacting: <br>- dehydration<br>- diarrheal disease & infections<br>- undernutrition<br>- maternal & newborn health<br>- poor mental health/psychosocial stress<br>- physical strain & injury<br>- violence |

# Vector ecology & health


|                         | IMPACT                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rising temperatures** | Vectors tend to prefer a warmer world<br>- rising temperatures accelerate viral replication, reduce incubation periods, increase biting, lengthen breeding periods & viral seasons<br>- changing meteorological conditions expand or shift geographic range of vectors<br>- precipitation extremes increase availability of standing water for mosquito breeding |
| **Zoonotic disease**    | New & increased opportunities for viral spillover<br>- geographic range<br>- population density<br>- pathogen spread<br><br>Increased pathogen transmission<br>- rates of infection<br>- pathogen load<br>- viral mutation                                                                                                                                       |

# Nutrition & Health

> Climate change impacts food availability, access, utilization and stability

| AVAILABILITY                                                                  | ACCESS                                              | UTILIZATION                                                                                      | STABILITY                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| - reduced food production<br>- disrupted food processing, storage & transport | - erosion of assets<br>- income loss & unemployment | - reduced diet quality (high CO2 levels reduce nutritional value)<br>- contaminated food & water | - market & price volatility<br>- political instability & conflict |

> [!ATTENTION] Food insecurity impacts infectious diseases, chronic diseases, altered immune responses & inflammation,  gut microbiome, and neurologic/mental health problems

# Future gaps & opportunities

| Research Gaps                                                                 | Opportunities for the Future                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Topical [[bias]] towards vector-borne pathogens                                   | Expanded topical focus to other disease types                                                 |
| Overrepresentation of temperate regions, high-income countries                | Expanded geographic focus to include tropical regions and those with more climate variability |
| Overemphasis on temperature-related exposures                                 | Expanded consideration of other climate exposures                                             |
| Dominance of single-focus, siloed, linear assessments                         | Multi-modal, global assessments to elucidate cascading risk pathways                          |
| Methodologicla emphasis on quantitative approaches                            | Mixed-methods research with qualitative components                                            |
| Preponderance of impact studies, less on adaptation and mitigation strategies | Solutions-focused interventions and cross-sectoral collaborations                             |
