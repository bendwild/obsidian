# Reflections

> [!PDF|234, 82, 82] [[Jennings CP The power of the policy brief (1).pdf#page=1&annotation=52R|Jennings CP The power of the policy brief (1), p.1]]
> > The pol- icy brief is a communication tool that wields great power and, if done correctly, can enhance sound and successful decision making about key and complex policy issues

> [!PDF|234, 82, 82] [[Jennings CP The power of the policy brief (1).pdf#page=2&annotation=58R|Jennings CP The power of the policy brief (1), p.2]]
> > Policy makers are indeed overwhelmed by the volume of information they receive and have a strong preference for informa- tion  that  is  concise  and  relevant  to  current debates.
> 
> 

Before setting up a policy brief, consider: 
- the source of request
- the problem or issue
- the objective
- the audience 
- the time frame

> [!PDF|187, 97, 229] [[Jennings CP The power of the policy brief (1).pdf#page=3&annotation=66R|Jennings CP The power of the policy brief (1), p.3]]
> > The policy brief is an invaluable tool that can effectively inform policy decision makers.  In  addition,  it  can  translate  policy research into a form that is relevant, timely, and useful to those on the front line of the policy pro- cess. Writing policy briefs is a skill that must be learned and cultivated by any nurse who works in the areas of health and public policy.
>
