> > In the context of emerging diseases, the existing IHR had proven ineffectual in forcing disease notiﬁcation for at least two reasons. For one, the limited list of reportable conditions—cholera, plague, and yellow fever—was of little relevance for the expansive category of emerging infections; second, there was no way to require countries to comply with IHR reporting requirements. Although, as Heymann put it, ‘‘in our emerging diseases program our idea was to change the culture so that countries could see the advantage of reporting,’’ a practical means of enforcing compliance was needed
