## Prerequisites to [[collective impact]]
3 conditions must be in place before **launching a [[collective impact]] initiative**
1. Influential champion
2. Adequate financial resources
3. Urgency for change
## Phases of [[collective impact]]
3 distinct phases of **getting a [[collective impact]] effort up and running**
1. Initiate action
2. Organize for impact
3. Sustain action and impact

![[Pasted image 20260304004754.png]]

> [!attention] Important to recognize: 
> - that the initiative must build on existing collaborative efforts
> - being realistic about the time it will take to get through these stages is essential

## Shared measurement systems
> [!PDF|234, 82, 82] [[Making collective impact work..pdf#page=6&annotation=264R|Making collective impact work., p.6]]
> > Practitioners consistently report that one of the most challenging aspects to achieving [[collective impact]] is shared measurement—the use of a common set of measures to monitor performance, track progress toward goals, and learn what is or is not working. 
> 
> 

> [!PDF|234, 82, 82] [[Making collective impact work..pdf#page=6&annotation=267R|Making collective impact work., p.6]]
> > Having a small but com- prehensive set of indicators establishes a common language that supports the action framework, measures progress along the common agenda, enables greater align- ment among the goals of different orga- nizations, encourages more collaborative problem-solving, and becomes the platform for an ongoing learning community that gradually increases the effectiveness of all participants

## Keeping [[collective impact]] alive
> [!PDF|234, 82, 82] [[Making collective impact work..pdf#page=7&annotation=270R|Making collective impact work., p.7]]
> > Two key structural elements enable [[collective impact]] initiatives to withstand the overwhelming challenges of bringing so many different organizations into align- ment and holding them together for so long: the  backbone organization  and  cascading levels of linked collaboration.
