# Reflections
Don’t think of an elephant! Whatever you do, do not think of an elephant. No one is able to do this. **Every word, like elephant, evokes a frame which can be an image or other kinds of knowledge**

Wow, very interesting about the framing of conservatism

Myths
1. The truth will set us free. If we just tell people the facts, since people are basically rational beings, they’ll all reach the right conclusions. 
2. It is irrational to go against your self-interest and therefore a normal person, who is rational, reasons on the basis of self-interst
3. Political campaigns are marketing campaigns where the candidate is the product and the candidate’s positions on issues are the features and qualities of the product
Compassionate conservatism
