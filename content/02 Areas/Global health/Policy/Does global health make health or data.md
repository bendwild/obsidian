title: Does global health make health or does global health make data?
---
Questions to ask yourself: 
1. Why more research, but not more health?
2. Who is “*x intervention*” really for? What data is being collected and for whom?
3. Are we just guinea pigs? 

**In listening to and learning from trans women, there is wisdom in their research mistrust.**
Hesitancy, mistrust, loss to follow-up, poor enrolment cannot be understood as a trait of trans women addressed solely through education about the research process (individual education intervention), but as a justified response to systemic inequities replicated by and manifested through te extractive logics of research. 

**The value driving extractive data practices**
Academic research begets more research, producing scientific knowledge as global currency. It is **OUR RESPONSIBILITY AS RESEARCHERS** to actively recognize our role and the part we play in the perpetuation of extractive data and the benefits received. 

[[HIV]] science cannot be divorced from an examination of the role that power and politics play in the perpetuation of extractive data practices.
