> Risk pooling turns **unpredictable individual risk** into **predictable collective cost**
> = combining risk and money for coverage later
> = helps with two risks (losing money, getting sick)

# Pooling design

| Pooling structure                       | Who bears risk           | Financial protection                             |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Single tax-funded pool (f.e. Beveridge) | Society as a whole       | Strong                                           |
| Regulated social [[health insurance]]   | Employed + contributors  | Moderate, depends on coverage of informal sector |
| Fragmented private                      | Individuals in each pool | Weak for the sick and poor                       |
| Predominantly out-of-pocket             | Households directly      | None                                             |
