# Why does the private sector exist in health systems?

1. Supply gap
	1. Public sector does not produce enough services so private fills the vacuum (common in LMICs)
2. Quality gap
	1. Public sector exists but patients prefer private (better *perceived* quality)
3. Demand gap
	1. specific population segments choose private for amenity, privacy, or speed


> [!INFO] Proponent argument
> 1. Efficiency (competition drives down cost and drives up quality)
> 2. Capacity (private investment expands total system capacity without public expenditure)
> 3. Choice (patients can select providers that match their preferences)

> [!ATTENTION] Equity risk: a two-tiered system (poor services for poor populations)
> - Wealthy, insured, urban population → access (higher quality) private care 
> - Poor, uninsured, rural populations → access (underfunded) public care 

# Conclusion

> The private sector is not necessarily the problem or the solution, it’s a fact in global health

However, you need to be smart with it: 

1. Regulate (set and enforce standards for private providers)
2. Contract (purchase private services on behalf of public payers, [[public-private partnerships]])
3. Integrate (bring private providers into referral pathways and information systems)
