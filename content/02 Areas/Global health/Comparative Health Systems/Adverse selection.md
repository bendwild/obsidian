> = when individuals with higher expected health costs are more likely to enrol in insurance than healthier individuals

Individuals know more about their own health than insurers do (information asymmetry) which makes:
-High-risk individuals have greater incentive to enrol
-Healthy individuals may opt out

|                      | Death spiral                                                                                                 | Cream skimming                                      | Cherry picking                                               |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Mechanism**        | 1. Healthy people drop out<br>2. Pool skews sicker<br>3. Premiums rise<br>4. Healthy people drop out<br>etc… | Insurers intentionally attract low-risk individuals | Insurers selectively enroll or retain profitable individuals |


> [!important] Policy solutions
> - mandatory enrolment (everyone must join, including health individuals)
> - community rating (prevents risk segmentation)
> - risk equalization/risk adjustment (transfers risk between insurers)
