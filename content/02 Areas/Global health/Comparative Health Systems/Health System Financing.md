> = how money is collected, pooled, and used to purchase health services

# Functions
1. Revenue collection (how money enters the system)
	1. Taxes
	2. Insurance
	3. OOP
2. Risk pooling (how financial risk is shared)
3. Purchasing (how money is used to pay for services)

## Revenue collection

|                                                  | Type    | What                                                                     | Characteristics                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Model                                | Examples                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **General taxation**                             | Public  | Government collects taxes → funds a national health service or fund      | - universal by design, everyone is covered<br>- risk is pooled across entire population<br>- equity potential is high, depending on how progressive the tax system is<br>- coverage is tied to citizenship/residence, NOT employment                          | ![[Pasted image 20260325110852.png]] | - UK<br>- Canada<br>- Scandinavia<br>- Thailand   |
| **Social health insurance**                      | Public  | Payroll contributions from workers and employers → dedicated health fund | - historically linked to formal employment<br>- coverage tied to contribution, not citizenship<br>- risk pooling depends on who is in the fund<br>- key equity gap (informal workers, unempoloyed, and self-employed often excluded)                          | ![[Pasted image 20260325111030.png]] | - Germany<br>- France<br>- Japan<br>- South Korea |
| **Private insurance**                            | Private | Individuals or employers purchase insurance from private companies       | - premiums set based on risk or regulated<br>- creates market incentives but also market incentives (adverse selection, cream skimming)<br>- risk pooling limited to insured group<br>- key equity gap, favors the healthy and wealth<br>- willingness to pay | ![[Pasted image 20260325111139.png]] | - US                                              |
| **Out-of-pocket**                                | Private | Patient pays directly at the point of care                               | - no pre-payment, no pooling<br>- least equitable (the sick pay most at the worst time)<br>- ability to pay                                                                                                                                                   | ![[Pasted image 20260325111253.png]] | - often dominant system in many LMIC              |
| **Donor funds/aid**                              | Other   |                                                                          |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |                                      |                                                   |
| **Medical saving accounts**                      | Other   |                                                                          |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |                                      |                                                   |
