> Most health gains in the past century came from **policy, delivery and financing changes

# The structural problem of innovation and equity
1. Donor-funded
	1. Vulnerable when funding shifts
	2. Many innovations collapse when the grants end (no revenue model built)
2. Market-based
	1. Gravitates towards purchasing power 
	2. Markets cannot distinguish **unmet need from inability to pay**
3. Cross-subsidy
	1. Works for some services but requires high volume and standardizable procedures
	2. Cannot be applied everywhere
4. Policy innovation
	1. Often most powerful and most neglected

> [!ATTENTION] Innovation alone cannot solve equity

# How to evaluate innovations
*Ask yourself three questions:*
1. Was the need validated (or assumed?) with the people who will use it? 
2. Is the business model viable without permanent donor subsidy?
3. Does it reach the poorest, or only those who can already pay?
