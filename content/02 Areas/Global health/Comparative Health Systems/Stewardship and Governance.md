|                                     | Stewardship                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Governance                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**                      | = the overarching **health system function** responsible for <br>- setting direction<br>- regulating behavior<br>- ensuring accountability                                                                                                                                     | = the **processes** through which decisions are <br>- made<br>- implemented  <br>- enforced                                                                     |
| **Question it is trying to answer** | What should the system do?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | How do decisions actually get made?                                                                                                                             |
| **Functions**                       | - sets vision and priorities (making sure the [[healthcare system]] aligns with its goals: health, responsiveness, financial protection)<br>- establishes and enforces rules<br>- aligns actors and incentives<br>- ensures accountability<br>- generates and uses information | - determines how decisions happen<br>- shapes who has power<br>- enables or constrains stewardship<br>- successful governance includes **performance feedback** |
| **Weak stewardship or governance**  | - fragmented financing<br>- poor regulation<br>- informal systems<br>- inequity<br>- poor performance                                                                                                                                                                          | - governance tensions (federal vs state, public vs private, political cycles)                                                                                   |

> [!info] Types of accountability
> - Vertical
> 	- citizens to government
> 	- through elections, public pressure, social movements
> - Horizontal
> 	- institutions to institutions
> 	- through parliamentary oversight, regulators, courts, auditors
> - Social
> 	- civil society to system
> 	- through civil society organizations, media, journalism, patient organizations
>
