# Goals
Health systems decide
- who gets care
- what care they get
- who pays

|                    | Health                                                                                     | Responsiveness                                                                                                                              | Financial protection                                                                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**     | Improving the level of population health AND reducing inequalities in health across groups | Provide healthcare and financial protection in a way that is responsive to the needs of those we are serving                                | Protecting people from financial hardship due to health care                                       |
| **Key dimensions** | - level of health (life expectancy, mortality)<br>- distribution of health                 | - respect for persons (dignity, autonomy, confidentiality)<br>- client orientation (timeliness, quality of environment, choice of provider) | - out-of-pocket (OOP) spending<br>- catastrophic health expenditure<br>- impoverishing expenditure |

# What shapes a health system?

> [!ATTENTION] Even the best-designed systems function under constraints
> 1. Money is limited
> 2. Workforce is limited
> 3. Capacity is limited
> 4. Demand is **unlimited**, demand for care will ALWAYS exceed supply
>    
> This means, that systems will have to make choices
> ![[Pasted image 20260328135150.png]]

1. Values
	1. What does this society believe about health care? Public good or private commodity? Citizenship right or earned benefit?
2. History
	1. What institutions already exist? Who controls them? What political coalitions built the current system and have interests in preserving it? 
3. Evidence
	1. What do other countries’ experiences suggest works? (Often secondary to values and politics in actual policy)
