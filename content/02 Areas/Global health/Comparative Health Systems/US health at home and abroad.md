> > **The impact of US domestic health on global health starts with which problems the USA takes seriously.** One of the clearest examples is [[HIV]]/AIDS. The fight for the US Government to adequately acknowledge and fund an [[HIV]] response and later effective [[HIV]] treatment in San Francisco, Los Angeles, New York, and across the country created a fierce activist community that  together  with  international  counterparts  and sympathetic policy makers helped extend access to that same hard-won antiretroviral treatment in sub-Saharan Africa and other lower-income nations.
