> = first-contact, continuous, comprehensive, coordinated care that integrates prevention, treatment, and health promotion *(Alma Ata 1978, reaffirmed Astana 2018)*

# Functions of primary healthcare
1. First contact 
	1. entry into system
2. Comprehensiveness 
	1. not just curative
3. Coordination 
	1. connecting to higher levels when needed
4. Filter demand
	1. less unnecessary specialty use
	2. more preventive care
	3. earlier detection
	4. lower cost per health gain

> [!IMPORTANT] Countries with strong primary healthcare have: 
> - better population health outcomes
> - lower costs
> - more equitable distribution of health
