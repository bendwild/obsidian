# Access

> = the opportunity to reach and obtain appropriate health care in situations of perceived need
> ≠ utilization or availability

## [[Supply and demand]] aspects of access

![[Pasted image 20260407140702.png]]


## Five dimensions of access to healthcare
1. Approachability
2. Acceptability
3. Availability & accomodation
4. Affordability 
5. Appropriateness

# Quality

> = the degree to which health services increase the likelihood of desired health outcomes and are consistent with current professional knowledge

# Three domains of quality of healthcare
1. Structure (inputs)
2. Process (what is done)
3. Outcomes (what results)

> [!ATTENTION] The **effective coverage** problem
> Access without quality = coverage that does not produce health

> [!INFO] HAQ index
> - the HAQ index measures a health system's ability to deliver effective care by looking at **mortality from conditions that should not be fatal if treated properly** 
> - the underlying assumption: if a country has good access to quality care, deaths from treatable conditions should be low
