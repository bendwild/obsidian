# Three core dimensions
![[Pasted image 20260422000921.png]]

| Population coverage                                                                             | Service coverage                                                                             | Financial protection                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who is covered?                                                                                 | Which services are covered?                                                                  | What costs are covered?                                                                             |
| = proportion of population with access to health services (enrollment, geography, eligibility…) | = breadth and depth of services included (preventive, curative, rehabilitative, palliative…) | = proportion of costs covered (goal to eliminate catastrophic and impoverishing health expenditure) |

> [!attention] Not all coverage is the same!
>- Legal/formal coverage (enrolled in a scheme or entitled by law)
>- Realized access (*actually* uses services when needed)
>- Effective coverage (receives high-quality care that changes health)

> [!INFO] Common lessons on the path to UHC
> 1. Political commitment and leadership are necessary
> 2. There is no single model to UHC
> 3. Expansion is rarely linear (policy windows open with political transitions or crises)
> 4. Revenue mobilization is a constraint in LMIC (pooling matters more than the source)
> 5. Equity requires deliberate targeting (passive expansion leaves the hardest-to-reach behind)
