> Essential medicines satisfy the priority healthcare needs of the population. Should be available at the most affordable price (or free).

# EML selection
Based on 4 domains: 
1. Disease prevalence
2. Public health relevance
3. Efficacy and safety
4. Cost-effectiveness

> [!INFO] Before 2002, affordability was a prerequisite
> - expert / opinion-based selection
> - affordability as a precondition for inclusion
> - this is why antiretroviral were excluded from the EML

# EML designation
Drives procurement adoption and measurable price reduction
- all UN agencies use the EML to guide drug donations and procurement priorities
- public insurance coverage in several countries is linked to EML status
- 94% of countries use EML as basis for public drug procurement
- EML inclusion signals clinical validity
