# Etiology
- Obstructed labor → obstructive fistula
	- common preventable cause of maternal and perinatal mortality/morbidity
	- commonly caused by sephalo-pelvic disproportion or malpresentation/malposition of foetus
	- treatment: operative delivery
- Iatrogenic fistula
	- increasing trend in caesarean section rates

# Epidemiology
- estimating incidence and prevalence difficult due to methodological challenges
	- rare and stigmatized condition
	- most recent estimates of 500 000 individuals, most in SSA
	- could be 50 000 - 100 000 new cases annually
