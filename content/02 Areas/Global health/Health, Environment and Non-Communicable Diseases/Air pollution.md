> [!ATTENTION] Air pollution exposure inequality
> People with lower SES have: 
> - greater exposures to ambient air pollution
> - disproportionate proximity to polluting land uses and toxic emissions
> - greater exposure to household air pollution
> - less health-promoting amenities

> [!IMPORTANT] Recommendations for preventing the exposure of toxic chemicals
> 1. Advocate for policies to prevent exposure to toxic environmental chemicals
> 2. Work to ensure a healthy food system for all
> 3. Make environmental health part of health care
> 4. Champion environmental justice
