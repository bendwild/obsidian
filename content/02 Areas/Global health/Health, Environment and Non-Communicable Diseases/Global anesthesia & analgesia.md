# Epidemiology
- 5.5 billion people (83% of world's population) have low to nonexistent access to opiates
- Majority of injury & oncologic disease burden occurs in LMICs
- Six HICs account for ~80% of global morphine consumption
- LMICs account for ~6%
- Significant disparities in practice in HICs (and SF!)


> [!ATTENTION] Major disparities
> - 150 countries lack reliable access to morphine
> - 5 billion people lack access to safe, affordable, surgical, anesthesia, or analgesia care
