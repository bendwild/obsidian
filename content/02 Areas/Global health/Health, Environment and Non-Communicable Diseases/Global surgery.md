# Surgery

> [!IMPORTANT] Access ≠ safe surgery & anesthesia
> - peri-operative mortality in Africa is twice the reported global average, despite an overall healthier patient population
> - surgery is a leading cause of deaths (7.7% of all deaths globally)
> - 30 million people face catastrophic health expenditure due to payment for surgery and anesthesia each year


# Misconceptions
1. Poor outcomes in resource-limited settings
2. Not cost-effective in resource-limited settings
	1. essential to economic growth
	2. value of lost output

# Challenges
1. surgical disease & pain overshadowed by infectious disease
2. historical focus on vertical programs
3. lack of concerted voice
4. need for new models (more than missions and equipment donations)

# Priorities
1. Anesthesia and surgery workforce
	1. get up to 20/100 000 
	2. focus on retention
	3. improve quality of care (more deaths due to poor-quality care than non-utilization of care)
2. Essential medicines and analgesia
3. Infrastructure
4. Advocacy
