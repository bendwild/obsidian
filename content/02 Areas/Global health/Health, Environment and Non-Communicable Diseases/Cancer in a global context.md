> [!ATTENTION] Cancer is widely perceived to be:
> - A disease of the rich or of wealthy nations (**cancer is also a disease of poverty**)
> - A disease of the elderly
> - A death sentence 
> - The consequence of informed behavioral choices 
> - Bad luck 
> - An indication of being on the “right side” of the epidemiological transition
> - Way too expensive and complex to treat (**cancer can be highly treatable in low resource settings**)

# Epidemiology
- Cancer kills more people worldwide than [[HIV]], [[malaria]] and [[tuberculosis]] combined
- 60% of new cancer cases and 70% of cancer deaths occur in LMICs (and numbers are rising)

# Prevention

> 40-50% of cancers could be prevented
> - [[tobacco]] control
> - vaccination (HPV, [[hepatitis]] B)
> - lifestyle (diet, nutrition, )
> - environmental toxins ([[air pollution]])

**SCREENING ≠ EARLY DETECTION**

|                | Screening                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Early detection                                                                                                             |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Definition     | = testing across a **healthy population**, to identify individuals with diseases, who **do not yet have symptoms**                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | = testing **as soon as first symptoms appear**                                                                              |
| Which cancers? | Only relevant to subset of cancer types:<br>- [[Cervixcarcinoma]]<br>- [[Borstkanker]]<br>- [[Prostaatkanker]]<br>- [[Colorectaal carcinoom]]                                                                                                                                                                                               | Relevant to all types of cancer<br>                                                                                         |
| How?           |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | - raise public awareness, reduce stigma<br>- train first-line providers<br>- strengthen systems for diagnosis and treatment |
| When?          | More complex public health strategy, should only be undertaken if: <br>- screening effectiveness has been demonstrated<br>- resources are sufficient to cover nearly all the target group<br>- facilities exist for follow-up, diagnosis, **and treatment**<br>- disease prevalence is high enough to justify effort and costs of screening | Early detection is priority in **regions where majority of patients are diagnosed at late stage**                           |
