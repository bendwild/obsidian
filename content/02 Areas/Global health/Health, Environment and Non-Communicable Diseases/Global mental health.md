> = aims to prevent and treat mental and substance use disorders around the world

# Inequity in GMH
- LMICs are home to > 80% of the population, but have <20% of mental health resources
- >75% of those with SMI in LMICs received no care at all (in sub-Saharan Africa the treatment gap can exceed 90%)
- Even where treatment is provided, often falls far below minimum acceptable standards
- Failure to provide basic necessities, unmonitored detention, shackling and chaining are well documented abuses -> *failure of humanity*

# Bridging the gap
- Increasing training for primary care specialties
- Collaborative care models
- Expanding number of specialty training programs
- Financial constraints
