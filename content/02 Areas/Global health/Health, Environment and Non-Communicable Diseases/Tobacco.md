> Factsheet: 
> - around 80% of the world's 1.3 billion tobacco users live in low_and middle-income countries
> - tobacco kills up to half of its users who don’t quit
> - tobacco kills more than 7 million people each year (including an estimated 1.6 million non-smokers exposed to second-hand smoke)
> - tobacco costs over $1.4 trillion USD / year, close to 2% of the global GDP ( 40% of costs in LMIC)

> [!INFO] Global voluntary target
> Reduction in 30% of prevalence by 2025

> [!attention] WHO FCTC (Framework Convention on Tobacco Control)
>- United Nations treaty
>- establishes tobacco control as a priority on the public health agenda
>- provides a political and legal platform for adoption of sound, evidence-based tobacco control measures
>- introduces a mechanism for firm country commitment and accountability
>- 183 Parties covering about 90% of the world’s population

> [!ATTENTION] Tobacco industry interference remains one of the greatest threats to the adoption, implementation, and enforcement of effective tobacco control measures
> → **Article 5.3 FCTC** Protection of public health policies from commercial and other vested interests of the tobacco industry

> [!attention]  The entire chain of tobacco production supply and consumption harms the environment “craddle to grave” 
> **→ Article 18 FCTC** The parties agree to have due regard to the protection of the environment and the health of persons in relation to the environment in respect of tobacco cultivation and manufacture within their respective territories
