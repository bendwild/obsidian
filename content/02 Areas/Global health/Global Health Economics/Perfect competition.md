Perfect competition is an economic theory that represents an ideal market structure where numerous buyers and sellers operate, leading to an optimal balance of [[supply and demand]]. 
# Assumptions
- **many producers and consumers**, none of whom has individual power over price
- producers try to **maximize profits**, and consumers try to maximize **utility**
- the good is **homogeneous** (market is not segmented on basis of difference of goods)
- **no barriers** to entry or exit
- all buyers and sellers have perfect **information**
