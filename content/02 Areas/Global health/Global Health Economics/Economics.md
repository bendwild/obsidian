= study of the **allocation of scarce resources**

Economic forces are pervasive.
- Scarcity is endemic.
- Society constantly fights to expand and reallocate the pie. 
- Economics provides framework (models) to understand these forces

Additionally, economists are influential (for better or for worse)
-> The purpose of studying economics is not to acquire a set of ready-made answers to economic questions, but to learn how to avoid being deceived by economists [[@Joan Robinson]])

# Important concepts

**Macro-economics**: the study of how an overall economy behaves

**Micro-economics**: the study of the allocation of scarce resources (behaviour of individual decision makers and specific industries)

**Econometrics**: statistics applied to econ

**Positive economics**: describe/analyze what “is”

**Normative economics**: what “should be”, positive economics + **value judgements**
