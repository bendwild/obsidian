| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Utilitarianism]] | Maximize the greatest amount of good for the greatest number of people                                      |
| Rule of rescue     | Identified lives get first claim over anonymous, statistical victims                                        |
| Urgent need        | Greater urgency = greater claim on resources                                                                |

| Equity             | Divide resources so outcomes are distributed as equitably as possible (reduce disparitie sin health status) |
| Prioritarian       | Resources provided to the least advantaged members in society                                               |
