# Summary 

|             | Demand                                                                                               | Supply                                                                           |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Definition  | The amount of some good or service that consumers are **willing and able** to purchase at each price | The amount of some good or service a producer is willing to supply at each price |
| Assumptions | 1. Utility maximization<br>2. Finite resources<br>3. Full information<br>4. Rational choice          | 1. Profit maximization<br>2. Constrained by inputs<br>3. Efficient production    |
| Curve       | ![[Pasted image 20260120211852.png]]                                                                 | ![[Pasted image 20260120211957.png]]                                             |
| Law         | = consumer demand **decreases** with price (increase)                                                | = producer supply **increases** with price (increase)                            |
| Cave        | No distinction between needs and wants                                                               |                                                                                  |

# Market equilibrium
= point where quantity demanded equal quantity supplied

![[Pasted image 20260120212249.png]]


|            | Moving along the curve     | Shifting the entire curve                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Demand** | - change in product prices | - availability of related goods (substitutes or complements)<br>- income (taxes, rebates)<br>- preferences (product recall)<br>- size of consumer base (aggregate demand) |
| **Supply** | - change in product prices | - price changes of production inputs (rise in gas)<br>- size of industry (competitors)<br>- technological innovation                                                      |
