## Planning for the future
1. Need-based = planning for **what the country will need**
2. Labor market demand = planning for **what the country can afford**

Then **adjust for supply** = planning for how many workers might be available given current training, recruitment and retention

## Predicting the future
1. SDG Benchmark Approach = plot health worker density and the proportion of SDG indicators achieved
2. Demand model (much more complicated)
	1. Demand = f(income, cost of services, health status)
	2. ln(physicians per 1000it) =   β0 + β1*ln(GDP per capitait-1) + β2*ln(GDP per capitait-4) +  β3*ln(GDP per capitait-5) +   β4*ln(Out of pocket health expenditures per capitait-2) +   β5*ln(Pop65it-3) + Country fixed effects + ξit
