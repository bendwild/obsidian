# Cross price elasticity
> measures the change in quantity demanded of good x in response to a change in the price of good y

= % change in quantity demanded of good X / % change in the price of good Y
- tends to be positive if two goods are substitutes (coffee and tea)
- tends to be negative if two goods are complements (coffee and milk)

> [!Example] Example
> The impact of e-cigarette and cigarette prices on e-cigarette and cigarette sales
