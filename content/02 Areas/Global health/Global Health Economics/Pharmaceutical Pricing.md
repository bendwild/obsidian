# Perspectives

| Pharmaceutical companies                                                                                                                                        | Healthcare providers                                                                                                     | Patients                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| = sellers<br>- price their product to cover the costs of drug discovery, development, manifacturing, marketing and further R&D<br>- provide return to investors | = purchasers<br>- at a price that offers good value for money<br>- be reimbursed and paid for the offered treatments<br> | = end-users<br>- access safe and effective drugs that are affordable |

# Drug pricing mechanisms

|                                              | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Rationale                                                                                                                                                             | Challenges                                                                                                                                                                   | Examples                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Health technology assessment**             | = evaluates value for patients and/or the health system<br>- utilitarian approach<br>- maximize efficiency in resource allocation                                                                                          | - limited resources<br>- need for standardized health metric to **assign equal social value** to an intervention<br>- overcome subjectivity and allocate funds fairly |                                                                                                                                                                              | - clinical benefit<br>- cost-effectiveness<br>- budgetary impact                                                          |
| **Value-based pricing**                      | = sets prices primarily based on **perceived value of a product**, rather than on the cost of production or historical prices<br>- pharmaceutical companies are reimbursed according to **perceived value** of the drug    |                                                                                                                                                                       |                                                                                                                                                                              |                                                                                                                           |
| **Internal reference pricing**               | = compares the prices of pharmaceutical products (therapeutically similar) for one another, **within a country**<br>- works best if countries have policies supporting the appropriate use of generic/biosimilar medicines | - enables consistent pricing across medicines with similar therapeutic effects<br>- encourages competition among these products                                       | - requires technical expertise<br>- resources needed to maintain price database and track price variations                                                                   |                                                                                                                           |
| **External/International reference pricing** | = informs price negotiations by calculating a reference price based on publicly available pricing data from one or more other countries                                                                                    | Ensure that the maximum price paid for a drug is not excessive (in comparison to countries that are comparable)                                                       | - low prices may undermine **availability of medicines** and lead to launch delays & product withdrawals<br>- downward price convergence can hamper investment in innovation | - EU<br>- variations in target drugs, prices used, selection of reference countries, calculation method, update frequency |

# Cost-Containment mechanisms

| Mark-up regulations                     | Payback                                                                              | Risk-sharing agreement                                                                                                                             | Price transparency                                                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| - additional costs are banned/regulated | - manufacturers need to refund once target budget/profitability treshold is exceeded | - financial/performance-based arrangements in which manufacturers agree to lower prices or provide refunds if prespecified targets are not reached | - information about drug prices is disclosed to relevant stakeholders/general public |


> [!attention] Why rise in drug prices?
> - market size / patient pool / R&D cost (priced higher to recover costs)
> - fewer new generics
> - price control (inadequate regulation induces price hikes)
> - lengthy patents (creates monopolies)
> - limited competition (low profit, large backlog of generic drugs waiting or approval, shortages)

# Policy Levers

| National Health System                                                                                                                                                       | Payers and Providers                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| - shorten brand-name market exclusivity period that allows manufacturer to establish prices<br>- new patent laws<br>- less segmented health system to allow for negotiations | - formulary management tools<br>- accountable care organizations<br>- mandated automatic substitutions to generics |


> [!info] Advantages and disadvantages of drug pricing regulation
> Advantages
> - majority of profit is used for marketing and administration costs
> - drug makers generally increase their prices every year
> - huge variation in drug prices
> - overnight rise in drug prices, unexpected burden
> - unjustifiable and unsustainable for medically vulnerable patient population and health care system
>   
>   Disadvantages
>   - cost of developing drugs is high
>    - only 3/10 drugs earn returns
>    - 11% of drugs that enter clinical trials are approved
>    - regulation reduced pharmaceutical revenues
>    - 1% increase in pharmaceutical revenue = 3% increase in annual number of new pharmaceuticals approved for use
