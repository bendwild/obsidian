The rational consumer: 
- has **stable preferences**
	- completeness (has preferences with every choice)
	- transitivity (if A>B & B>C, then A>C)
- always **wants more**
- prefers a **mix of goods** over extremes
- maximizes **utility**
- has complete **information**
