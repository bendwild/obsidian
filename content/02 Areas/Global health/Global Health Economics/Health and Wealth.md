# Wealth → Health

## Cross-sectional data
= strong association of **wealth and longevity**
![[Pasted image 20260209163828.png]]

Higher incomes promote health through
- improved nutrition
- better access to safe water and sanitation
- increased ability to purchase more and better-quality health care
- improved in-utero and early-life environment that can have long-term health effects
- …


> [!ATTENTION] Economic growth necessary but not sufficient for health improvements!


## Experimental data
1. Gertler, Paul. 2004. "Do Conditional Cash Transfers Improve Child Health? Evidence from PROGRESA's Control Randomized Experiment." American Economic Review, 94 (2): 336–341.

1. Role of improved nutrition and chronic illness (increased life expectancy associated with increased height)
