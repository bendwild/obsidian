> [!INFO] What?
> = comparison of value for money of alternative health programs 
> - one-dimensional measure
> - non-monetary outcome (life years gained, infections avoided)
> - results expressed as **incremental cost per effect gained**
> - allow for comparison **within disease areas**   

# 1. Frame the problem
1. Define the research question
	1. Target population, choice of alternative options?
2. Define the time horizon
	1. Often requires a lifetime horizon but not always
3. Define the perspective
	1. Viewpoint from which economic evaluation is conducted)

# 2. Identify the relevant costs and benefits
1. Direct costs and benefits
2. Indirect costs and benefits
3. Intangible aspects

# 3. Measure the different costs and benefits
1. Choose appropriate physical/natural units
2. Adjust for timing of costs and consequences (discounting)
3. Think of the perspective taken
4. Relies on good understanding/description of the alternatives considered

# 6. Handle uncertainty
[[Economic evaluations]] are subject to uncertainty 
- could a change in the value of any of these variables reverse the conclusions?
- how sensitive are the results to changes in the values of key parameters?

| Types of uncertainties         | Definition                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Methodological uncertainty** | Uncertainty according to approach<br>- which approach to economic evaluation should be used?                                                                        |
| **Parameter uncertatinty**     | Unexplained, [[sampling]] variation surrounding the input parameters<br>- what are the exact values of the parameters within the model?                                 |
| **Structural uncertainty**     | Uncertainty about the assumptions that underline the model<br>- does the model adequately capture the relevant characteristics of the disease and the intervention? |
