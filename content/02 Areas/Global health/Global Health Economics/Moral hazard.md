= tendency for insurance against loss to **reduce incentives to prevent or minimize the cost of loss**
= inefficient

| Ex ante moral hazard                                                                               | Ex post moral hazard                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Behavior changes that occur **before** an<br>insured event happens and make that event more likely | Behavior changes that occur **after** an insured event happens and make recovering from that event more<br>expensive              |
| A person with comprehensive [[health insurance]] is less careful about preventive behaviors            | After falling ill, a patient with [[health insurance]] consumes more healthcare services (extra doctor visits, tests, or procedures), |

> [!IMPORTANT] Moral hazard is a misleading term for a rational response that **DOES** follow market dynamics

> Solution = cost sharing
