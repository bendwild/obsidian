# Reflections
- Stop saying we’re all in the same boat. While we’re all weathering the same storm, we’re not in the same boat. 
- This crisis has broadly separated us into the exposed poor and the shielded rich [[@Anjana Ahuja]]
- [[Pareto principle]] to quality improvement
	- can’t sustain the whack-a-mole approach
	- can’t spend so much money and time to improve such narrow targets
	- small progress in quality improvements over the past decades
	- lack of concrete progress applies to entire biomedical enterprise, not just quality improvement!
		- much clinical care is an expensive band-aid for adverse health effects of socioeconomic and public health problems
		- f.e. 30-100 patients have to take statins for 10 years just to prevent one event (not perse death), is this worth the billions of dollars while little money is given to the etiological problems
- No “magic bullets”
	- classical quality improvement interventions only improve outcomes marginally
- Personalized medicine based on social determinants of health
	- we can already predict individual risk and differences in life expectancy - with a subway map
- **We have confused improving healthcare by improving health**
	- health is the places we live, work, the food we eat, the amount of money we have, our education etc
- We need to do far more work in meso and macro fields
- QI projects need to improve more than one of the quadruple aims or have multiple targets
- If we in healthcare don’t focus on improving population health, who will
- Radical solutions might sometimes be the most realistic
- The problems we are facing are not bugs, they are part of the design of the system
