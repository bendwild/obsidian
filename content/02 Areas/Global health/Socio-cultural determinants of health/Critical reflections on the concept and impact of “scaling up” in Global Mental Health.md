> > To increase access to services, GMH prior- itizes  “scaling up”  mental health services, primarily advocating for the export of Western centred and developed biomed- ical and psychosocial  “evidence-based”  approaches to the Global South. While this emphasis on scalability has resulted in the increased availability of mental health services in some LMICs, there have been few critical discussions of this strategy.
