= detailed document that outlines **estimated project costs that will support a project**

# Why?
- feasibility assessment
- resource allocation
- credibility with funders
- financial oversight

# How?
By setting up a *research budget*, that includes: 
- **direct costs**
	- personnel costs (and fringe benefits)
	- consultant costs
	- participant costs
	- [[data collection]] & analysis
	- publication costs
	- equipment
	- supplies
	- travel
	- training costs
- **indirect / facility and admin / overhead costs**
	- clerical fees
	- facility usage
	- …
- budget justification
	- how will the funds be spent and how does it justify the costs?
