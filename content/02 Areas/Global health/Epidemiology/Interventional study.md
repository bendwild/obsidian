- **investigators allocate exposure**
- at least 2 arms/groups
- ideally, subjects are **randomized** into exposed and unexposed
- the outcome of subjects is known/measured at baseline
- subjects will be followed over time to detect changes compared to baseline


> [!ATTENTION] Benefits of randomization
> - characteristics (**both known and unknown**) should be evenly distributed across treatment and no treatment group
> - level of [[Confounders]] in the treatment group are similar to placebo group
