> = disease of the optic nerve

# Symptoms
Causes defects in **peripheral vision**

| Open angle glaucoma                                | Angle closure glaucoma                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| = chronic disease<br>- asymptomatic until too late | = acute or chronic disease<br>- severe pain, red eye<br>- asymptomatic until too late |
| More common if family history and African descent  | More common in East Asians                                                            |
# Diagnosis
- intraocular pressure
- visualization of optic nerve **(vertical cup-to-disk ratio)**
	- ophthalmoscopy (direct observation)
	- photography
	- optical coherence tomography
- structural tests to assess optic nerve
	- visual field testing
- visualization of anterior chamber angle
	- gonioscopy (direct observation)
	- optical coherence tomography

# Treatment

| Open angle glaucoma                                    | Angle closure glaucoma                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| - eye drops<br>- oral medicine<br>- laser<br>- surgery | Acute<br>- immediate referral (blindness within hours)<br>- laser or surgical iridotomy<br>Chronic<br>- laser or surgical iridotomy<br>- oral medicine (= open angle)<br>- check intraocular pressure at eye hospital |
