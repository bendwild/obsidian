> = light is not focused in the right place

[[Epidemiology]]
- all ages
- increasing prevalence in Asia

Symptoms: 
- blurry vision

Risk factors
- for myopia: near work

Diagnosis
- Refraction
	- manifest refraction (trial lenses)
	- autorefractor
- Pinhole
	- if vision improves with pinhole, suggests vision could improve with spectacles
