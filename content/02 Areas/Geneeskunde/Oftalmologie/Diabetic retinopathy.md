Pathophysiology: 
- microvascular complication of uncontrolled diabetes leads to hemorrhages, retinal swelling and retinal neovascularization

Risk factors: 
- [[Diabetes mellitus]]

Symptoms: 
- asymptomatic early
- blurry vision later

Diagnosis: 
- dilated retinal examination at least once per year
	- ophthalmoscopy
	- fundus photography

Treatment: 
- strict control of blood sugar
- laser
- intraocular injections of [[Corticosteroïden]] and VEGF inhibitors
