[[Epidemiology]]: 
- most common cause of blindness in resource-limited settings

Causes: 
- old age
- trauma
- [[Diabetes mellitus]]
- [[Corticosteroïden]]

Symptoms: 
- gradual onset of blurry vision

Clinical examination: 
- penlight (only advanced cataract)
- slit lamp examination

Treatment
- no medical treatment
- surgery at eye hospital 
	- inexpensive
	- one-time cure
	- low complication rate
	- extremely cost-effective
