# History
- Evidence of TB in Europe, Americas, and N. Africa since prehistoric times (~70,000 years ago)
- Reached epidemic proportions in Europe in the early 1600’s
- Leading cause of death in Europe from 1600-1900’s

# Epidemiologie
- wereldwijd enorm prevalent (each year TB is responsible for more deaths worldwide than [[HIV]])
- in België 3.6/100 000

# Transmissie en ziektverloop
## Transmissie
- Large droplets (heavy, quickly falls to the ground)
	- if inhaled, impacts upper respiratory tract
	- **never reaches the alveoli**
- Small droplets (break apart to become smaller *droplet nuclei*)
	- remain airborne indefinitely
	- once inhaled, droplet nuclei can easily reach alveoli
	- MTb stable in droplets because of their thick cell wall

## Mycobacterium tuberculosis (MTb)
- Small rod-shaped bacteria (bacilli)
	- **penetrate deep into lung**
- Thick lipid ([[cholesterol]]-rich) cell wall
	- **resistant to hostile environments**
	- **resistant to most staining (diagnostic) techniques
- Aerobic (O 2 dependent) 
- Multiplies slowly - every 1824 hours
	- **resistant to most antibiotics**
	- **requires long treatment times**
- Can remain dormant for 0.2 – 0.5 μm (microns) in decades

## Diagnose
- Rx: infiltraten 
	- **CAVE 10% normal**
- Microscopie: auraminekleuring met zuurvaste staven
	- staalkwaliteit belangrijk!
	- **poor sensitivity** 
		- 1 specimen = 30% sensitivity
		- 2 specimens = 50% sensitivity
	- smear-positive patients 10x more likely to be infectious vs smear-negative TB patient
- Kweek: **meest gevoelige techniek**
	- wordt vaak samen met microscopie ingezet
	- lower limit of detection 10-500 bacilli/mL sputum (still misses TB cases)
		- 1 specimen = 85% sensitivity
		- 2 specimens = +/- 100% sensitivity
	- duurt 8w & resource-intensive
- -Moleculaire testen 
	- zeer snel, al na +/- 2u antwoord
	- RIZIV-terugbetaling sinds 2017
	- bijna obligaat, echter geen info over eventuele resistentie
		- GeneXpert MTB/RIF
			- endorsed by WHO in 2010
			- PCR based test
			- POC test
				- results in 90 minutes
				- sensitivity 70%
				- **also detects MDR-TB**
			- expensive
		- Xpert Ultra (2nd generation assay)
			- higher sensitivity than GeneXpert (overall sensitivity **comparable to a single culture**)
			- no difference in detection of MDR-TB
			- no difference in sensitivity among PLHIV
		- Urine lipoarabinomannan (LAM)
			- cheap (6 USD), easy (urine), fast (25 mins)
			- allows for same-day TB treatment initiation
			- true POC (no lab or highly-trained staff needed)
			- sensitivity highest for PLHIV with low CD4 counts

> [!INFO] Ideal test characteristics
> - highly sensitive
> - rapid (POC)
> - inexpensive
> - non-sputum base (less risk for nosocomial infection)
> - low infrastructure and training requirements

> [!attention] Importance of effective TB treatment
> - protect the community (decrease in droplet nuclei/day)
> - cure the individual
> - prevent drug-resistance

> [!ATTENTION] CAVE MDR-tuberculose (multi-drug resistant)
> Risicofactoren zijn: 
> - voorafgaande behandeling
> - gekend contact met MDR-tuberculose
> - immigratie
> - [[HIV]]-infectie
> - leeftijd <65j, geslacht M
> - gedrag & levensstijl (dakloosheid, IV druggebruik, ethylabusus)

> [!attention] Biggest threat to successfull treatment is non-adherence!!


# Preventie

| Primary prevention                         | Secondary prevention                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| = prevent TB infection                     | = prevent *development* of TB disease                             |
| - respiratory (N95) masks<br>- BCG vaccine | - TB preventive therapy<br>- TPT + ART (if [[HIV]]+)<br>- BCG vaccine |

> [!INFO] What does the BCG-vaccine do?
> - not great at preventing TB infection
> - even worse at preventing reactivation of TB
> - **does reduce risk of severe TB disease in kids younger than 5 years**
