# Climate Changes & Health Disparities

Unequal climate-health risks for vulnerable group
- more severe climate change impacts
- inability to cope with, recover from & adapt to these impacts
- higher risk of exposure to these impacts
- highest mortality related to climate change among the lowest emitters
